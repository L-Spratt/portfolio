01 — Specific Users

Crew Anomaly Investigation System

Astronauts on future Mars missions will have to wait 44 minutes to get a response from ground control. C.A.I.S gives the crew the autonomy they want to solve onboard problems themselves instead, and meets the specific needs of these highly-trained and habitual users.

Client
NASA
Role
Engineering, design, research

02 — Secure Environment

Care2Care

UPMC is a hospital that categorizes the needs of patient caregivers and recommends resources accordingly. Care2Care is a digitzed version of this manual process, built end-to-end with both a mobile and web component, and built securely to protect private information and integrate into their existing infrastructure.

Client
University of Pittsburgh Medical Center
Role
Engineering

03 — Accessibility

ChefMate

ChefMate is designed as a companion for older adults in the kitchen. ChefMate carefully addresses the array of possible accessibility considerations, such as motor difficulties, hearing loss, or low vision, all while maintaining the user's autonomy.

Client
Carnegie Mellon University
Role
Design, engineering

04 — Empowering

Comtrade

This project included a deep dive into the realm of trading card games and concert ticket sales, and the pain that scalping brings to the common consumer. Comtrade aims to address this by placing the power in the hands of people who are actually involved in the hobby.

Client
Carnegie Mellon University
Role
Research, engineering

The vehicle went through numerous physical iterations through multiple rounds of testing as common expectations and behaviors began to surface.
